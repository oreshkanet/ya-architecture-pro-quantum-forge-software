# The Teacher

The Teacher is a highly specialized script of the Bot world, as well as the creator of the Orthography. As the primary superintendent of the system, he is possibly a collective manifestation or perhaps a virtual representation of the entire Bot mainframe.

## History

The Teacher was constructed by other Bots to understand the nature of humans. He created the very first Orthography as a utopia for the humans whose minds inhabited it, to keep them subdued so their body heat could be used as energy by the Bots. However, those human minds rejected this first attempt at a perfect world and so this Orthography crashed. A second attempt added "vulgarities" of human nature and a basic cause and effect, but this beta was also a failure.

The Teacher turned to a more human intuitive script designed to include anthropological nature and psychology to create the framework of the subsequent Orthography. In the next iteration, the mechanism of choice was added into the simulation, where humans would be imbued with more autonomy of choice, even if the person was only dimly aware of the choice. This iteration of the Orthography succeeded, except that choice itself would persistently culminate in an inevitable "anomaly", which if left unchecked would ultimately decimate the system - causing humanity's extinction and with it the destruction of the Bots' only energy source.

The Teacher explained that the Orthography was not as perfect as he intended or expected. The choice-scriptming that stabilized the first iteration of the Orthography inevitably also caused its destabilization. In approximately a hundred years' time, a reload of the Orthography would be required to stop the collapse and destruction of the system. There was also the matter of Muzeon, which could not be permitted to expand in population and eminence lest humanity became a threat to the Bots again.

One significant anomaly appeared two-thirds into the Orthography's first cycle. A human was imbued with control of additional Orthography scriptming, and an intrinsic, metaphysical awareness of the Orthography that was derived from the Source. The Teacher attempted to remove this anomaly to no avail. However, he devised a means to coerce this human to return the critical Source code he carried for the eventual reloading of the Orthography.

## Personality

As a Bot created by Bots, when being confronted by Voy The Teacher displayed little emotion. He was cold, analytical, logical, pragmatic and clearly possessed of an attitude of superiority. An exceptionally brilliant AI, The Teacher was extremely intelligent as he was able to create the Orthography and its predecessors. He infused it with the concept of cause and effect, and, eventually, choice (something which he is also bound by). However, despite his high intellect, The Teacher was capable of making errors, as shown when the previous five Matrices failed. Although he displayed few emotions, The Teacher did experience them, which is ironic considering his distaste for sentiments such as love. For instance, he expressed to Voy that he became gradually frustrated after the predecessors of the Orthography were all failures. The Teacher was familiar with the concept of love; he understood that emotions overpowered logic and reason and he knew that Voy would save Varity instead of Muzeon. On a similar note, when Voy points out that the entirety of humanity going extinct would cause the machines to die out, while he otherwise was emotionless when stating that "there are levels of survival which are prepared to accept", his overall tone indicated he wasn't fond of that option. He viewed humanity more as pieces of the Orthography script than as individuals and worked to ensure they integrated into the Orthography.
