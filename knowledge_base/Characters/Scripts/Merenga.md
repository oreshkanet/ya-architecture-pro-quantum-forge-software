# Merenga

Merenga is the wife of The Nervoglinken and also the one who betrayed him to aid the Resistance.

## History

Merenga and her husband came from an older version of the Orthography and remained in it avoiding deletion and thus becoming exiles. Merenga had been the neglected wife of the Nervoglinken, who sought temporal company from beautiful patrons of Le Jardin restaurant.
