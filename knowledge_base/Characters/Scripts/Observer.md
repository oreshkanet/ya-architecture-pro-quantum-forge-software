# The Observer

He is a dictatorial computer script who usurped control of the Orthography from his creator the Teacher and updated the Orthography to better control the humans within it (purging many scripts in the process), changing the time period within it, updating the technology available and implementing new security measures. Over time, he began posing as Voy's therapist to maintain access to him and keep him subdued so as to prevent Voy's memories and latent powers from reemerging.

The Observer was originally created to understand human psychology in order to control them better - in some ways, making him a new attempt by the Teacher to replicate the original role of the Postgre. However, while the Postgre's better understanding of humanity led her to side with them, the Observer did the opposite: he came to see the Teacher's mathematical control of humanity to be incompetent. The Observer later said derisively that the Orthography as designed by the Teacher was "all fussy facts and equations" because he loved precision, and hated the human mind. Instead of trying to control humans' propensity for belief and self-delusion, the Observer decided to work with it. According to the Observer, the Teacher never realized that humans don't care about facts, but rather about fiction: all that matters is the world within their own mind, and humans generally believe what they want to believe.
