# The Pilot

The Pilot is an exile who created and operates Soul Road and is a servant of another exile script known as the Nervoglinken.

After saving fellow Resistance member Buhel, Zarube is approached by the Pilot in a subway station. While Zarube does not know who this person is, the Pilot tells her that Muzeon lasted 72 hours the last time it was attacked by the Bots. When questioned about his identity, the Pilot states that he is only an observer of the events unfolding.

Later, the Pilot is approached by Necromantus, Jiraph, and Varity onboard a train. When he sees them approaching, he pulls out his gun and tells them to leave. Jiraph tries to explain that they require his help but he refuses. Instead, the Pilot pulls the emergency brake lever and flees the train, the three of them in pursuit.

As they enter another subway platform, the Pilot turns around and fires at his pursuers, who duck behind pillars. He checks one of his many watches and realizes that he is just in time to catch the train fast approaching them. As the train approaches, he fires his gun at the three of them until its empty and jumps across to the other side of the platform. He disappears behind the train, his next stop being Soul Road.
