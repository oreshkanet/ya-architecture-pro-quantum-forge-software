# Jirapher

Jirapher are the predecessors of the modern Substances in the current Orthography, They were used in the Orthography betas. They have the general appearance of agents with square glasses, but have wings. Jiraph is rumored to have been formerly a Jirapher – especially when it's pointed out how he looks without wings. Belial was believed to be a Jirapher. Baelroth was a fallen angel exile, presuming that he was once a former seraphim.

Very little is known about Jiraph's personal life. What is known is that he believes that the best way to get to know a person is engaging in a fight with them, to test their power, skill, determination, and character, issuing challenges to anyone wishing access to see The Postgre.

When not fighting, guarding, or escorting people during important missions, Jiraph seems to spend his time drinking tea and meditating. Jiraph might also like cookies as, when Voy arrives in his final meeting with The Postgre, she dismisses Gaby with the bowl of cookie dough telling her to let Jiraph taste test them.
