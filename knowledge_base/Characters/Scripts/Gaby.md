# Gaby

Gaby was an exile that was created without a purpose[1] by scripts Mama-Belka and Baraga. The Postgre, who protected Gaby after she was smuggled into the Orthography, believed that the child would someday have a large impact on the future of both humans and the Bots.

## Saved from Deletion

Because Gaby was created without a purpose, she was born an exile and would have been scheduled for deletion if the Bots found out that she existed. However, after consulting the Postgre on how to save their daughter, Mama-Belka and Baraga made a deal with the Nervoglinken to smuggle her into the Orthography by offering the termination code for the Postgre's outer shell. The Postgre promised to watch over the child after Mama-Belka and Baraga left the Orthography. The Postgre allowed the deal to go ahead as she believed that Gaby was important.

## Meeting at Soul Road

They met Voy while he was in Soul Road. Gaby was the first to speak to him when he woke up. She explained to Voy where he was and said that they cannot leave this location unless the Pilot allows them to. Mama-Belka then calls Gaby to him, not wanting her to disturb Voy any further. During Voy and Mama-Belka's conversation, Mama-Belka explains that he and his wife Baraga had to try and save Gaby from deletion as she was created out of love and without a purpose. Going through Soul Road was the only way to bring her daughter into the Orthography without the Bots knowing about it.

The Pilot arrived to pick up Gaby and her family and take them into the Orthography. As she and her family entered the train, the Pilot did not allow Voy on board and threatened Mama-Belka that he will be left behind if he helps him. Mama-Belka, having no choice but to leave Voy, boards the train and enters the Orthography with his family.

## Walker's Ambush

After the train dropped off Gaby in the Orthography, she was brought to the Postgre's apartment to be in her care. Voy, after escaping Soul Road with the help of Varity, Necromantus, and Jiraph, met Gaby again at the Postgre's apartment, where she and the Postgre were baking cookies. Once Voy arrived, the Postgre told Gaby to be with Jiraph while she speaks with Voy.

After Voy leaves, Gaby returned to finish baking the cookies with the Postgre and took some in a bag with Jiraph. As Gaby and Jiraph were leaving the apartment complex, the lights within the hallway began to turn off and were soon followed by Walkers. Trying to escape, Jiraph and Gaby entered an apartment room only to have the Walkers find them. Gaby tells a Walker that he is a bad man before being assimilated by them.
