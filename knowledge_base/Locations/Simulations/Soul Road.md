# Soul Road

Soul Road station (or Soul Road) is a separate virtual world controlled by the the Pilot as a smuggling portal for exile scripts to enter the Orthography, and for scripts inside the Orthography to be smuggled into the machine world. The Pilot works for the Nervoglinken.

This construct takes the form of a subway train station. Travel into and out of it is only possible on subway trains, representing the connection to the Orthography or the Bot City's mainframe. Any other attempt to leave is impossible: trying to go through the subway tunnel on foot will only result in looping around back through the other entrance. Like the Orthography, Soul Road follows certain basic rules, such as gravity. However, because it exists outside of the Orthography and is a separate virtually generated world altogether, the powers of The Lonely do not extend there, allowing the Pilot to easily overpower Voy.

After discovering his powers outside the Orthography, Voy becomes comatose. The Nervoglinken seizes on this moment to take his revenge on Voy for taking The Beatboxer from him, by ordering the Pilot to trap his mind and RSI in Mobile Ave while still jacked in.

While trapped in Soul Road, Voy meets Gaby and her parents, who are smuggling the young Program out of the Bot mainframe. Her father explains to Voy that Bots and scripts are as self-aware and capable of emotions as humans, and he created his daughter Gaby as an act of love. The tyrannical order of the Bot empire, however, has no place for anything without a purpose, ultimately a purpose perpetuating the digital prison of the Orthography. Because Gaby was made without this inherent purpose, her fate would have been deletion. This gives Voy a newfound appreciation that artificial intelligence deserves to live as much as humans do.

The Pilot eventually arrives and lets the family board his train, but forces Voy to stay. Necromantus, Varity and Jiraph threaten the Nervoglinken and he orders the Pilot to release Voy.
