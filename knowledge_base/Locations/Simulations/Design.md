# Design

The Design was a virtual work space created to run simulations. It was also used as a loading script to launch or create virtual objects in preparation for whenever Muzeon operatives hacked into the Orthography.

## Appearance

Created by Resistance members, the Design, upon load, initially appeared as a dimensionless, empty, white arena. Once some scripts were launched however, the Design's simulated reality could be made generally indistinguishable from that of the Orthography, and could be made to follow or break from the basic rules of physics. It came with an integrated decoder, which allowed the activity within the simulation to be watched as a video feed on the operator's monitors, in the same manner as a security guard can watch a closed-circuit security camera feed,[1] and as opposed to interpreting the green lines of code that were getting logged when connected to the Orthography itself. Those who entered the Design appeared already clothed but the outfit could also simply be changed within the Design.

## History

Necromantus used the Design to educate Voy on the truth about the Orthography, showing him what the Earth actually looked like in their present time. Not long after, he challenged Voy to "show (him)" the skills he had learned via his headjack uploads, using simulations to assess Voy's progress. Later, in preparation for rescuing Necromantus from Substances in a heavily-fortified government building, Voy and Varity entered the Design to gather the weapons and bombs needed for their rescue mission.

Monkey used the Design in a less wholesome fashion, mostly for sexual encounters with the woman in red, whom he created. Peter and Goe of the Bodarus combined business and pleasure in their sparring script, while Qos had her loyalty tested while in her favorite simulation.

The Design was also used by Zarube and Brost in preparation to retrieve the emergency message from the Bodarus, as well as when Brost readied to spar with Varity.

60 years after the events of the original trilogy, a resurrected Voy was jacked into the Design by the crew of the Insomnia, who were desperate to keep him alive. In the Design, Necromantus tells Voy of the events after The Orthography Modernizations and duels him.
