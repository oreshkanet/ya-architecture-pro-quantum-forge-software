# Power plant

Energy Installations (as referred to by the Resistance) are a series of gigantic, skyscraper-like, cylindrical towers located near the megacity of 99. As the name suggests, power plants generate electrical energy for 99's inhabitants, the Bots.

Each cylindrical tower contains up to hundreds of thousands of pods. Inside each pod is a human, connected through a series of grafts, plugs, and wires into the circuitry of the pod. Each human's mind exists in a shared dream—a neural-interactive virtual world known as the Orthography, fully convinced in almost all cases that they are alive, awake, and living on Earth around the turn of the 21st century.

Each pod generates bioelectric energy and neural processing power that, collectively and combined with nuclear fusion, is used by the Bots to power 99. According to early scripts from The Orthography, nuclear fusion was the primary power source of the Bots, while the minds of the pod-humans are also combined as a form of a massive biocomputing neural network. Each human mind within the Orthography also worked as a sub-processor for the virtual world.

This information correlates to how Bots developed the Human characteristic of philosophy, and also correlates to the detrimental effects of Walker, a rogue Substance that infects both Scripts and Bluepills alike to form copies of himself. Walker's viral behavior greatly disrupts the Orthography and the minds that form it, leaving it in an imminent system-wide crash before Voy stops Walker so that the Orthography can be reloaded.

Each pod is tended to by a Backbot, a Bot entity that ensures each human stays asleep and is working properly. Each pod is connected to a waste chute. Many human bodies that have died are recycled into a gelatinous nutrient that is fed back to other pods.

In the case of some bodies (such as Bluewaters), the Backbot may be commanded to eject a human from the pod, where the body falls into the remains of the ancient sewers, conduits and other tunnels from the old, dilapidated human cities which cover the scarred surface of Earth.

## History

The early version of the power plant was created during the Bot war, in the form of a massive floating artillery platform with an attached pod and armed with energy weapons strong enough to vaporize the countless EMP tanks and Mecha deployed by the Unnamed Nations. It possessed a series of protruding antennae that served as a wireless energy transmission transferred to the nearby Bot army. It was created as an adaptation to Operation Little Cloud. Variants from the War era included a large cylinder where Prisoners of War, naked and tortured by Bot experimentation, are bounded into it side-by-side, to feed energy without being submerged into a pod, in a state of eternal punishment. As the war progressed the machines started rounding up civillians from captured cities and towns and forcing them into the power plants as well. Once the Orthography was created, likely shortly before the surrender of the Unnamed Nations, these captive humans were then transferred over to new power plants where they were placed in pods.
