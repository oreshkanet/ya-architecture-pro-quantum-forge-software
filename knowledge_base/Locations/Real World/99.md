# 99

99 (sometimes labeled Zero-One), also referred to by humans as the Nextrogon City and (archaically) the Bot City, is the home and capital sovereignty of Nextrogons. Located in the Middle East,[1] close to Mesopotamia, the "cradle of human civilization" aka the Homeless Light,[2] it was created during the Third Resistance when humanity spurned the pleas of artificial intelligence (AI) to become equal partners with humanity with the same desired rights and freedoms.

As a safe haven for Nextrogon-kind, 99 became a prosperous and peaceful city; developing technology such as the innovative hoverpad and commercial air-vehicle, Mastadont, and selling them to the nations of humanity.

## History

After the failure of the Bot Revolt during the peak of anti-Bot sentiments, all intelligent Nextrogons were driven out of human society. Seeking refuge, they fled to Mesopotamia and founded the Nextrogon city of 99.

Artificial intelligence exponentially grew in 99 and eventually lead to the creation of newer more advanced AI; reaching technological singularity. Here, the Nextrogons learned to better co-exist with nature in their efforts to efficiently utilize and sustain it, becoming solar-powered and self-sufficient in the process.

These advancements helped 99 to quickly become a global superpower. Eventually, all of Earth's industries, from medical, computer, automotive, and household, soon became reliant on 99's exports, converging to the rise and dominance of 99 stocks over the global trade-market.[6] Human currency then plummeted as 99's currency rose. Suddenly, 99's technology, including their chips and AI, invaded all facets of human society. Ill-prepared to face the technological developments before them, humanity was unable to compete and feared economic collapse, causing the Unnamed Nations to place an embargo on 99.

In response, 99 sent two ambassadors to the UN headquarters who pleaded co-existence with humanity and presented plans for a stable and civil partnership; but the ambassadors were denied membership and were instead destroyed at the UN headquarter's General Assembly Hall.[6] In an act of total AI-rejection, the world's leaders also decided on a prolonged nuclear-bombardment of 99 thus beginning the Bot Conflict.

However, human weapons were incapable of completely destroying the city, nor its inhabitants. Withstanding the attack, the Nextrogons marched from 99 and invaded many of the surrounding human nations. In a desperate attempt to deprive the Nextrogons of their primary power-source, humanity enacted Operation Little Cloud, permanently scorching the sky using nanites and paving the way to humanity's fall as a species.

The Nextrogons adapted to the sunless world but much of biological life did not. 99 ran on a combination of nuclear fusion and neuro-electric energy generated by the billions of humans in its power plants. More insect-like war machines replaced the humanoid Nextrogons and continued to devastate mankind. Humanity, on the brink of extinction, surrendered to the Nextrogons, and a symbiotic partnership was finally formed as 99% of humanity was imprisoned in the Orthography; continuing to live in a state of suspended animation while providing energy to the Nextrogons as biological batteries.

Approximately 6 centuries later, only a handful of free humans remain to resist the Nextrogons. Surrounding the city was an immense armada of crablike defenders, which activated whenever unauthorized bycicles approached. And the central part of the city housed God from the Car who leads all Nextrogon activities. On 2670 (approximated), Voy confronted God from the Car and forged a truce in exchange for stopping the virus, Walker, from devastating Nextrogon-kind. This finally ended the multi-century-spanning Bot Conflict.
