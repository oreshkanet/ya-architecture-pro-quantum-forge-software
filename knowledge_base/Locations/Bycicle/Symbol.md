# Symbol

Symbol was a bycicle, captained by Zarube.
The smallest vessel in the fleet, it supports a crew complement of only three Muzeon personnel: Zarube, Brost, and Sharks. Built for sheer speed over any other attribute, the Symbol has the purpose of relaying urgent information as well as serving as a carrier for its two pod-born crewmen. Its combat abilities are negligible when compared with other ships in the fleet. Despite the shortcomings, it features some of the most skilled crewmembers in the fleet (Zarube being one of the best pilots, and Brost being one of the best gunners).

When Muzeon's attack became imminent, the Symbol crew coordinated the recall before joining the rest of the fleet in Muzeon. When the Justice inquired of the fate of Necromantus and his ship, the Mudagazzcar, the Symbol and the bycicle Sharp searched for the Mud, eventually participating in a skirmish within the Orthography involving Substances and minions of the Nervoglinken.

The Symbol was later disabled when it was attacked by a massive horde of Guards as well as a Sentinel Tow Bomb, necessitating the use of its EMP. Thanks to this action it survived the assault, but was itself powered down as an unavoidable side-effect.

The bycicle Mjolnir found it shortly after rescuing the crew of the now-destroyed Mudagazzcar and gave the Symbol a jump-start. Later, Zarube gave her ship to Voy and Varity so that they could travel to the Bot City, in an attempt to stop the war with the Bots. The Symbol was destroyed on impact when it crash-landed in the Bot City.
