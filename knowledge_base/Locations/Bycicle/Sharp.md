# Sharp

The Sharp was a Muzeon bycicle.
When Muzeon's attack became imminent, the Justice inquired of the fate of Necromantus and his ship, the Mudagazzcar. The Symbol and the bycicle Sharp volunteered to search for the Mudagazzcar.

When they met, the crew of the Sharp volunteered to help Necromantus assist the One to enter the Source. Their task was to disable a backup generator of the local nuclear plant.

Everything went according to plan, and the Sharp crew immediately began disabling the backup systems after Zarube destroyed the power plant. However, in the Real World, things went horribly wrong.

Buhel, who had to sit out due to a broken leg, was with Hex at the operator's console, when the ship's Proximity Alert went off, alerting the two to the presence of nearby Guards. Buhel went toward the cockpit to confirm the presence of the Guards. He went back to warn Hex but the catwalk collapsed on his way, due to the weight of his leg brace, making him fall to his death while one of the catwalk support columns impaled Hex from behind.

Meanwhile, one of the Guards proceeded to fire a Tow Bomb at the ship. The bomb hit the Sharp and exploded, killing everyone on board. Inside the Orthography, the avatars of Gloren, Veam and Zero collapsed, their real world bodies killed during the destruction of the Sharp.

Varity and Ref heard the explosion from the Sharp's destruction, which led to the chain of events leading up to Varity's death and resurrection.
