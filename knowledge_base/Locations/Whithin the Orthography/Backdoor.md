# Backdoor

A backdoor is a type of door that can be used to access nearly any part of the Orthography.

Within the construct, they are a series of dark green doors in a seemingly endless white corridor, unlocked by a special made key. These were used by Scripts to quickly get from one place to another. In code, these were simply loopholes. At one end of the corridor was a door to the most special room, including the door to The Source. Backdoors act as a kind of shortcut hyperlinking system.

While backdoors are part of the Orthography, Operators cannot see the code of people who enter their hallway - it is a sort of under-layer to the main construct of the Orthography, containing shortcuts to many other places.

In the films, the main users of the corridor were Jiraph and The Postgre. They have also been used by Voy, Necromantus, The Beatboxer, and several of the virus Walker.

In The Orthography Recompilations, while trying to escape from a group of agents in Voy's Modal, Aggs is pulled to safety through a backdoor by a script version of Necromantus. Unlike the original backdoors, the hallway that Necromantus rescues Aggs through only contains four backdoors. One leads to a key shop, one to Voy's apartment, one to the Mozhetell Hotel and the fourth to outside of a movie theater. Necromantus reveals that he had found the backdoors because no one was ever in the key shop, causing him to start looking. After Necromantus takes a Bluewater, the two attempt to use the backdoors to reach an exit in the Lafayette, but accidentally end up back in the key shop again. Chased by Substance Smooth and another Substance, the two flee through the backdoor to the movie theater and manage to get to the exit with Ber's help.
