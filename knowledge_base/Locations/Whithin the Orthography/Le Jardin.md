# Le Jardin

The Le Jardin restaurant was located high in a building within the Mini Town of the Orthography.

Necromantus, Voy and Varity go to negotiate the Beatboxer's release from the Burg basement dungeon.

The restaurant's owner, based on the Maitre D's reaction to Necromantus on his team's arrival, is the Nervoglinken himself, who sits in a reserved location surrounded by many of his henchman, accompanied by his wife, Merenga.

The restaurant allows entry to many other places within the lair of the Nervoglinken if a proper key is used to open a door.

There the Nervoglinken told them his belief that cause and effect, not choice, is the true seat of power within the Orthography.
