# Burg

The Burg was The Nervoglinken's mansion, located in the Mountains, a remote mountainous area resembling the Alps.  Voy has to fly about "500 miles due south" to save Necromantus and The Beatboxer in Mini Town.

The Nervoglinken and Merenga had access to it through manipulating the doors of Le Jardin.

The Beatboxer was imprisoned there by The Nervoglinken. After making a deal with Merenga, she led Voy, Varity and Necromantus to the mansion to retrieve The Beatboxer. Then, she killed Cibes and told Kail to go and notify The Nervoglinken.

When The Beatboxer was freed, The Nervoglinken showed up. The Doubles went after The Beatboxer who ran away followed by Necromantus and Varity and made their way to the Burg's garage. Meanwhile Voy easily fought, and defeated The Nervoglinken's henchmen.

When the fight ended, The Nervoglinken left and closed the door of the Château. Voy opened it and found himself in the Mountains. After Necromantus and Varity left with a car, Voy looked for another exit. Voy saw a Twin who grinned and closed the door between them. Voy smashed it and again ended up in the Mountains.

He decided to fly away and made it just in time to save Necromantus and The Beatboxer during the pursuit.
