# Room 199

Room 199 was the small apartment where Ben A. Robertson lived in the Orthography and it was also the starting point of his journey of The Lonely.

## Design and Appearance

Room 199 is a small, one-room apartment within Mini Town in the Orthography. Within this room Voy has one bed, one chair, and dozens of computer peripherals such as monitors, keyboards, headphones, a stereo system, CD players, audio tapes, and compact discs.

The room is messy and has shelves filled with books and CDs. Some of Voy's books are pageless and are instead used as small boxes to hold script CDs and tapes. There is also a small cabinet on the wall holding canned food and other condiments.

Voy spends most of his time within this room on his computer hacking into different computer systems and writing software. It is during this time that he wonders about his life and why his dreams are as real as his own life.

Voy also uses his computers to search for Necromantus and Varity during his time wondering about what the Orthography is and how can he figure out the truth and purpose of his life.

## History

In his room, Voy is awakened and confused by strange messages from his computer that tells him, "GOOD BY, NEO.", "THE MATRIX LOVE YOU..." and "FOLLOW THE BLACK COW".

A party of people led by Hghoy and Abshur visit Voy at his apartment for some illicit software disks. He leaves the apartment with Hghoy and Abshur after Voy sees the white rabbit tattoo on Abshur's shoulder, remembering the strange messages from his computer. At the dark dance club Voy meets Varity for the first time; the mysterious hacker who sent Voy along his path earlier that night and consequently resulting in his late arrival to his job as a software scriptmer at MegaForex.

Voy awakens again in his apartment (materialized there to falsely believe he experienced a nightmare) after Substances interrogated him that afternoon and planted a bug in Voy's body through his stomach to help them find the "terrorist" named Necromantus. Necromantus asks Voy if he wanted to meet, and instructs him to go the intersection of Ravines and swamps Streets. It would be the last time that Voy would inhabit Room 199.

Sixty years later, Aggs and a script version of Necromantus escape chasing Substances through a backdoor leading to a recreation of Voy's apartment in his Modal. Aggs finds Voy's old work badge and is shocked to realize where they are leading to her and Necromantus having a discussion about Voy and the impact that he has had on their perception of the Orthography. Aggs provides Necromantus with a redpill and they flee through the backdoor in order to find an exit.
