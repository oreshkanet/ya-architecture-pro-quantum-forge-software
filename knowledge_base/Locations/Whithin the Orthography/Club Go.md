# Club Go

Club Go is a nightclub run by The Nervoglinken, a ruthless, powerful elder script that exists across the different versions of the Orthography.

The club is located in the basement level of a building in Mini Town. The entrance is guarded by armed supernatural scripts with gravity-defying capabilities.

The club is a depiction of a fetish nightclub, filled with stereotypical members of goth, punk, and other modern subcultures. Some of the patrons even appear to be engaging in heterosexual and homosexual acts of a sexual nature. It is similar to the club in which Voy first met Varity. The patrons are Exiles in fact, and their attire and mannerisms are tied into the supernatural beings they are supposed to be emulating.

Necromantus, Varity and Jiraph defeat these guards before proceeding to the inner chamber. Here they meet with The Nervoglinken to barter for Voy's escape from Soul Road station, a computer construct run by The Nervoglinken's henchman, The Pilot. After Varity starts a fight and creates a Mexican standoff with The Nervoglinken, Voy is freed.
