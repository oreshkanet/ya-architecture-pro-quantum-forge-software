# Mozhetell Hotel

The Mozhetell Hotel is used by Necromantus and his crew as a headquarters, and their main exit point is there.

Voy was taken there to meet Necromantus and it was there that he took the redpill.

It is discovered by the agents when Number drops his activated cell phone in the trash can when they're going to the Postgre. Whilst walking to exit Voy experiences déjà vu, Panzer calls Monkey and tells him to get out but Monkey is killed when he is waiting in an upper level of the hotel alone. The agents take over Lafayette hotel.

Sixty years later, while trying to escape from the Orthography, Aggs tells a script version of Necromantus that they need to reach the Lafayette where there is an exit waiting for them. The two attempt to use the backdoors in Voy's Modal to reach it, but Necromantus' disorientation after taking the redpill causes them to first end up back in a keyshop with Substances and then outside of a movie theater. Using a portal provided by Ber, the two are able to reach the exit, a skylight, by skydiving out of a nearby window.
