# Insomnia

# History

Aggs rose to become captain of her own ship, and assembled a like-minded crew who never stopped believing that Voy was still alive but trapped in the Orthography, even after everyone else gave up hope years ago. They also remained dedicated to freeing more humans from the Orthography, when as the years passed General Zarube wanted to focus more of the resistance's resources on rebuilding their settlements in the real world.

# Design

The Insomnia was built decades after Muzeon's bycicle such as the Mudagazzcar, therefore there are numerous design upgrades both large and subtle throughout the ship - even the chairs look sleeker than the ones on the Mud.

The most important aspect of its design is that it was constructed with the help of free Synthients that switched to the humans' side, and as a result it has a hybrid design between old Muzeon bycicle and Sentinels. The forward half of the ship has hoverpads like an older human ship, but the rear section ends in a glowing red "tail" that sways back and forth, like the armor on a Sentinel.

Concept art also confirms that the Insomnia has a "stealth" mode, aiding it in penetrating into enemy power plants.

Behind the scenes videos clarify how the ship can be newer and more advanced while also having a nameplate stating it was built long ago: the base hull was originally an old Muzeon bycicle that was downed at some point and abandoned, but many years later PII found the hulk and salvaged it. They then gutted it and mostly rebuilt it.
